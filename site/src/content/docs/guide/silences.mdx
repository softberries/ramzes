While a silence is active, the events it matches are still recorded in the feed but sent nowhere. The
Silences page is where you create one, end one early, and see which ones apply. How a silence interacts
with routes and open notifications is covered in [Noise control](/ramzes/concepts/noise-control/).

## Creating a silence

<Steps>

1. Press **New silence**.

2. Under **Which events**, say what to silence. The fields are the same as a route's conditions:

   - **Topic**: an exact topic, or a pattern in which `*` matches any run of characters, as in `orders.*`.
   - **Minimum severity** and **Source**.
   - **Add label condition**: a label key that **equals** a value, **matches** a regular expression, or
     **is present**.
   - **Expression**: an optional test over the event. See
     [Expressions and templates](/ramzes/reference/expressions/).

   An event must meet all the conditions you fill in. A line under the fields repeats them in words,
   for example "Silences: topic infra.disk.\*". At least one condition is required: a silence cannot
   cover every event.

3. Under **For how long**, choose **30 min**, **1 h**, **4 h** or **24 h**, or **Until a date and
   time** and fill in **Ends at**. The form starts on 1 h. A date and time is in your browser's time
   zone and must be in the future.

4. Optionally write a **Comment** saying why, up to 500 characters. It is shown in the list and as the
   reason on notifications the silence holds back.

5. Press **Create silence**.

</Steps>

A silence created here starts at once. It can last at most a year.

### Silencing from a notification

The **Silence** button on a notification opens this form already filled in with the topic and every
label of the event that opened the notification. Loosen it before creating, for example by removing a
label that is unique to one event. See [Notifications](/ramzes/guide/notifications/).

## The lists

The three buttons above the table switch between silences by when they apply. Each carries a count.

| List | Holds | Starts sorted by | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Active** | Silences that apply now. | The one ending soonest first. | **End now** |
| **Upcoming** | Silences that start later. | The one starting soonest first. | **Delete** |
| **Ended** | Silences that are over. | The one that ended last first. | **Delete** |

The form on this page always starts a silence now. An upcoming one exists only when it was created
through the [admin API](/ramzes/reference/api/) with a start time.

The columns:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Events silenced** | The conditions in words, with the comment underneath. |
| **Ends**, **Starts** or **Ended** | For an active silence, "ends in 1 h, at 22:51". For an upcoming one, when it starts and for how long. For an ended one, how long ago. |
| **Created by** | The email address of the person, "API token", or a Slack user name followed by "(Slack)" for a silence made with the [Slack app](/ramzes/channels/slack/) button. |
| **Created** | When it was created. |
| **Actions** | **End now** or **Delete**. |

The search box ("Search conditions, comment or author") filters the list in view. The lists re-sort
themselves every 15 seconds, so a silence that ends moves to **Ended** without a reload. The page holds
up to 500 silences.

## Ending and deleting

- **End now** sets an active silence's end to this moment. It moves to **Ended**, and matching events
  are sent again from then on. Events that arrived while it was active stay unsent.
- **Delete** removes an upcoming or ended silence from the list. Neither button asks for confirmation.

When there are no silences at all, the page says "No silences, so every event that matches a route is
sent."

## Seeing what a silence held back

A notification that a silence suppressed is listed under the **Not sent** view of
[Notifications](/ramzes/guide/notifications/), with "silenced:" and the silence's comment as the reason.
In the [Feed](/ramzes/guide/feed/), filter by the outcome **Not sent**. The route tester on
[Routes](/ramzes/guide/routes/) also takes active silences into account and names the one that applies.
